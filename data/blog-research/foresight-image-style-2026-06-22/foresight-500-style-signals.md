## Subtitle Lessons

- Subtitle works as a standfirst: one concrete second sentence, usually 12-28 Chinese characters or one compact clause.
- It adds tension instead of repeating the title: why this matters, what changed, or what makes the title surprising.
- Best examples use one number/entity/date or one vivid consequence, then stop.
- Avoid CMS-summary prose; the standfirst should make the next paragraph feel necessary.
- For multilingual output, localize the tension and rhythm instead of literally translating the zh-Hant subtitle.
