## Durable Editorial Rules

- H2/H3 are section subtitles. They must be compact, reader-facing, and specific to the article argument.
- Good column titles usually combine entity + tension + implication; weak titles merely describe the topic.
- Keep column bodies to 4-6 major sections unless it is a true feature. More sections should mean more editorial assets, not more template labels.
- In-article images must be placed by explicit markers or separated across the argument; two generated images cannot sit together.
- Save compact style signals only. Do not copy or store full third-party articles.
